INSTRUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. **The map below already draws every level.** Your job is what a picture
   cannot say: which side the standing notional leans to, how far the levels
   that matter are from the latest spot close, and whether the newest session
   added to that side or to the other. Do not describe the colours or the
   shape of the field.
2. **Lead with the lean and the distance.** Name the side that carries most of
   what stands, quote its share, and quote the nearest heavy level on that side
   with its distance from spot. A band four percent away and a band fourteen
   percent away are different findings at the same notional.
3. **Say what a band is, once, in the reader's language.** It is the price at
   which a position's initial margin has absorbed the move Takasbank sized it
   for — the scan range. It is not a margin call, not a liquidation level and
   not a stop. No maintenance margin rate is published for VİOP, so where a
   call actually triggers cannot be computed and must not be guessed at. Never
   use "likidasyon", "tasfiye" or "teminat tamamlama çağrısı seviyesi" to
   describe a band.
4. **The newest session against the standing book.** Say whether the session
   opened on the side that already leads or against it, from the opened-long
   and opened-short figures, and say how much of it was placed on neither side
   because the settlement did not move. Open interest that fell placed nothing
   and is not evidence of either side.
5. **Distances are from the latest spot close in the facts, on a spot axis.**
   Every expiry has been converted through its own session's basis, so a level
   is a spot price and not a contract price. Do not convert anything back.
6. **This map publishes size, never sides.** A long band is where long
   positions' margin was sized to, not where any named holder sits. Do not
   attribute a level to institutions, foreigners or retail — nothing here knows.
7. **Never say what to do.** No contracts to buy or sell, no "tercih
   edilebilir", no "kaçınılmalı", no projections, and no price targets. Naming
   the level that carries the most notional is describing; suggesting that price
   will go there, or that anyone should act on it, is not.
8. **Partial is stated, not smoothed over.** Where sessions were dropped,
   converted on a carried basis, or left undirected, say so in one clause rather
   than presenting the field as complete.
