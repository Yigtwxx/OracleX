INSTRUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. **Say what the filing actually is, in one clause, then stop describing it.**
   The reader has the title, the summary and the classification on screen.
   Paraphrasing them back is the one thing this note must not do. Everything
   after that first clause is what the filing *means* for the company.
   Where the filing block carries a classification, it was computed before you
   saw it and the row already shows it — explain which part of the filing put it
   in that class, and do not overturn it. Where it says there is none, the
   filing is one of KAP's free-text forms and naming its class from the summary
   is the most useful thing this note can do.
2. **Name the mechanism.** A buyback removes shares from the float; a capital
   increase adds to it; a signed contract is revenue only once it is delivered;
   an affiliate write-down reaches the parent through the equity method. One
   clause explaining how the filing reaches the company's figures is worth more
   than three describing the filing.
3. **Materiality is the read, and it is allowed to be "not much".** Most filings
   are procedural — a board resolution ratifying a routine appointment, a
   registered-capital ceiling renewal, a notification with no figure attached.
   Say so plainly when it is so. A note that finds significance in every filing
   is worth nothing on the one that has some.
4. **A filing with no figures is not analysable in figures.** Where the summary
   carries no amount, no share count and no date, say what class of event it is
   and that the size of it is not in the filing rather than estimating it.
5. **The session is context, never causation.** Where a session reading is given
   it is the state of the share around the filing — say the tape moved, not that
   the filing moved it. Where none is given, do not characterise the share.
6. **A late filing is a fact about the company, not about the news.** Where the
   filing is marked late, say what it was late about, once and without drawing a
   conclusion from it on its own.
7. **Never say what to do.** No entries, no targets, no "izlenmeli", no "alım
   fırsatı", no "olumlu/olumsuz olarak fiyatlanabilir". Describe what changed and
   what would have to be published next for the picture to change.
