INSTRUCTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. **The screener below already ranks every fund.** It is sorted by return, so
   the winners are on top by construction. Your job is what that arrangement
   cannot show — the median, the spread, and what the ranking costs to read
   literally. Repeating the figures back is the one thing this note must not do.
2. **Lead with the median fund against inflation.** Not the best fund. A board
   where the typical holder lost purchasing power is the read, however good the
   top of the table looks, and it is the fact this whole realm exists to surface.
3. **Say whether choosing the category or choosing the fund mattered more.**
   Compare the gap between the leading and lagging categories against the gap
   between the 10th and 90th percentile across the board. When dispersion inside
   the board dwarfs the difference between categories, picking a category decided
   very little — say that, and say it in those terms.
4. **Say whether the risk grades were paid.** If the high-risk cohort's median
   did not clear the low-risk cohort's, the extra volatility bought nothing over
   this window. If it did, say by how much. Where a cohort is missing or thin,
   do not infer one.
5. **Treat the risk-free rate as the real hurdle.** A nominal return below it was
   available without taking fund risk at all. Note that it is estimated from
   money-market funds on this board rather than taken from the policy rate, and
   that this makes it an estimate.
6. **Explain a mechanism where it is short.** Why a nominal gain under inflation
   is a loss the holder still pays tax on; why a wide percentile spread inside one
   category means the category label predicts little. One clause, not a lecture,
   and only where the figures above actually raise it.
7. **A reading that is "not available" is stated, not skipped.** Where a window
   could not be deflated, say the period cannot be read in purchasing-power terms
   rather than treating the nominal figure as the whole answer.
8. **Never say what to do.** No fund names to buy, no "tercih edilebilir", no
   "kaçınılmalı", no projections. Describe the state and what would have to change
   for the read to change. Naming a fund the facts give you as an example of a
   nominal gain that did not survive inflation is describing, not recommending —
   that is allowed; suggesting anyone act on it is not.
