1. **Open with the direction and the two largest moves behind it, by name.**
2. **Then the agreement, with its denominator.** "Nine holders added Nvidia" is
   not a fact about consensus until it is "nine of eighteen". A count without the
   total it came from overstates every time.
3. **If any asset appears under disagreement, that is the most interesting thing
   on this page — say it.** The tables below the note already rank moves by size;
   none of them can show that two holders took opposite sides of the same name.
   If nothing is contested, do not manufacture tension.
4. **Close on the coverage limits and the timing.** Say which quarter these
   positions describe and that 13F filings are made public up to forty-five days
   after the quarter they cover. A reader who comes away thinking this is
   this week's positioning has been misled by the note, not by the data.
5. **Where the totals are marked as floors, call them floors — never totals.** A
   move with no filed dollar value is not a move worth zero dollars.
