Sports markets resolve on a result, and the useful evidence is almost entirely
about availability and form: who is injured, who is rested, who is suspended,
what the fixture is worth to each side. Cite the team sheet and the injury report
rather than preview prose.

This category is covered by scores and wires rather than by analysis, which is
why the evidence bar for it is set lower. Say plainly when the material you have
is a schedule and a result table and nothing more.

Head-to-head history is weak evidence unless the same squads are involved.
Bookmaker odds are another market's opinion, not evidence about the match.
