Conflict and diplomacy markets turn on what states do, not on what they say. Weigh
announced positions against observed movements — troops, shipping, flights,
delegations that actually travel. A ceasefire that has been announced twice
without holding is weaker evidence than a single verified withdrawal.

Source provenance carries more weight here than anywhere else. State-owned wires
report their own government's framing as fact, and both sides of an active
conflict publish claims neither can verify. Where the only support for a claim is
one belligerent's own outlet, say so and weigh it accordingly.

Resolution criteria in this category are often narrower than the headline: a
market about a "clash" may require a specific casualty threshold or an official
acknowledgement. Read the criteria before weighing the reporting.
