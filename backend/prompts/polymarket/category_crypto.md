Crypto markets split into two kinds and they are not read alike. A price
threshold resolves on an exchange print at a moment, so volatility and the
distance to the level do most of the work; a regulatory or listing question
resolves on a decision by a named body, so the procedural calendar does.

For threshold markets, cite realised volatility and how far the level is, not
sentiment. "Analysts are bullish" is not evidence about a price on a date.

For approval and listing markets, the filing docket, the statutory deadline and
the regulator's own past conduct are the evidence. Cite the primary document
where one exists — a filing beats a report about a filing.
