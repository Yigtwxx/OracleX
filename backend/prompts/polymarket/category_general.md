This market did not fall into a category with its own wires, so the evidence
comes from broad news and may be thin. Read the resolution criteria closely
before anything else: markets that end up here are often ones whose question is
unusual, and the criteria frequently turn on a definition rather than on an
event.

Weigh how findable the subject is. A question about a scheduled, reported event
with no coverage is telling you something; a question about an obscure subject
with no coverage is telling you only that it is obscure. These call for different
confidence and should not be treated alike.

Where the evidence is a single source, say so rather than building a reading on it.
